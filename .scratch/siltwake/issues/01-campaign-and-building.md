## Question

What should approximately ten hours of play contain, and how much building depth belongs in fixed habitats versus the vehicle fleet? Preserve the user's discovery-first, mystery-driven priorities, useful starting pod, island exploration, and increasingly capable craft. Define duration as an experience target without padding it with travel or resource inflation.
