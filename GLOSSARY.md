# Siltwake game concepts

Shared vocabulary for the ocean-world design interview. These definitions describe concepts; feature scope and balancing remain in the decision tickets.

## Language

**Home pod**: The player's initial refuge, with item storage, crafting facilities and a means to recover health.

**Mobile home**: A submersible with a usable interior where the player can live and conduct expedition preparation.
_Avoid_: Calling every vehicle a mobile home.

**Blueprint**: Discovered knowledge of how to construct or improve an item, vehicle or facility.
_Avoid_: Using blueprint to mean the physical resources needed to build it.

**Scan entry**: A discovered encyclopedia record about an organism, material, environment or artifact.

**Journal entry**: A record left by a person in the world that reveals their experiences or part of the unfolding mystery.
_Avoid_: Using journal entry for every objective reminder or scanned fact.
