# Siltwake — research brief for the later game-design critic

Research date: **2026-10-17**. Specification checkout: `c0bc4fcae7a0238b532bcac9b9dd56b4e5c0494a`. This prepares a dedicated, fresh, non-author **Astra at xhigh** critique after M3 / early M4. It is **not that critique, a playtest, milestone acceptance, or release approval**. No game runtime was inspected. The spoiler canon was not opened; this report contains no Siltwake reveal.

The most useful review unit is an expedition: **a player question → preparation choice → readable place or ecological rule → commitment → a return that changes knowledge or future options**. That is a Siltwake synthesis, consistent with [STORY_DESIGN](../STORY_DESIGN.md#tie-every-important-expedition-to-the-world), rather than a research result proving the game enjoyable. The critic should trace this relationship through actual play, including the middle and ending, instead of equating content counts or attractive screenshots with a working campaign.

Research used HTTP and PDF text extraction, without a browser. Ten primary sources below were inspected directly; existing citations were only leads. Developer accounts establish intent, reported changes and design reasoning, not independently measured effectiveness. No source establishes an optimal fear/relief ratio, a universally acceptable travel time, or ten hours of Siltwake play. Accessibility guidance informs examination of the existing brief; it does not silently impose a new certification or platform commitment.

## Primary-source findings and their transfer limits

**S1 — Resource geography and crafting work.** Charlie Cleveland, Unknown Worlds, **“New loot system and tech tree design,” 2015-02-24**. [Developer article](https://unknownworlds.com/en/news/new-loot-system-tech-tree-design); sections beginning “There were a few basic problems…” and “Here's a summary of the biggest changes…”. Full article inspected; attribution also confirmed by its official Steam republication.

**Sourced:** Cleveland identifies unclear resource locations, abstract names, near-identical recipes, inventory juggling for large builds and single-use intermediates that add work without choices. His revision associates resources with recognizable contexts, keeps old areas relevant, removes some intermediates and uses others to condense bulk or open branches. **Siltwake inference:** inspect whether a player learns *where and why* to collect membrane, crust or lattice, and whether a carrier component changes logistics or is merely another click. **Limit:** the historical design also uses randomized mineral contents and creature materials. It does not justify introducing random critical yields or mandatory predator hunting into Siltwake. Habitat identity need not mean one exhaustible deposit.

**S2 — Mission-specific equipment.** Hugh Jeremy / Unknown Worlds, **“Seamoth Update Released!”, 2015-09-10**. [Official announcement](https://steamcommunity.com/games/264710/announcements/detail/405677533508080906); full publisher text inspected through the [Steam news API](https://api.steampowered.com/ISteamNews/GetNewsForApp/v2/?appid=264710&count=100&maxlength=0&feeds=steam_community_announcements), matching that announcement ID. Relevant paragraphs: upgrade panel; nonlethal defense and its battery cost; sonar/storage; “Going cave exploring?” versus hostile-resource collection.

**Sourced:** the developer describes different loadouts for different missions and an energy cost for defensive action. **Siltwake inference:** assess craft and mount choices by the route, cargo, observation or safety they change. A higher depth number alone does not establish a role. **Limit:** one configurable Subnautica craft is not evidence that Siltwake's six hulls are balanced, nor a reason to import its weapon or socket counts.

**S3 — Threats tied to behavior, with salvage.** Unknown Worlds, **“Silent Running Update,” 2017-04-28**, publisher account Hugh. [Official announcement](https://steamcommunity.com/games/264710/announcements/detail/3819571572130893407), full text via the same Steam API. Sections **“Rig for Silent Running,” “Sonar Contact,” “Raise Shields,” “Abandon Ship.”**

**Sourced:** operating modes trade speed against noise/light and attack likelihood; active terrain sensing and passive threat sensing serve different purposes; decoys and shields support defense; a destroyed carrier leaves equipment/modules to salvage. **Siltwake inference:** a sound hunter should support an observable warning → changed player behavior → disengagement relationship, and failure should lead to an intelligible expedition. **Limit:** this historical wreck-salvage claim does not prove recovery from every depth or repeated total fleet failure. Siltwake's disabled hulls, protected advanced materials and Bobbin route are stronger, independently specified obligations.

**S4 — Refuge required iteration.** Unknown Worlds, **“Voice of the Deep Update,” 2017-06-21**, publisher account Hugh. [Official announcement](https://steamcommunity.com/games/264710/announcements/detail/1980870483552153883), full text via the Steam API. Section **“Silent Running 2.0.”**

**Sourced:** after feedback, the team reports a second pass with stronger shields, larger energy reserves and engine shutdown providing a silent safe haven. **Siltwake inference:** inspect whether tension can actually end at a refuge and whether a ship that represents home remains dependable. **Limit:** this is a historical balance revision, not a current-mechanics guide or proof of an ideal difficulty level. It does not authorize making every stopped Siltwake hull invulnerable. Only this non-spoiler balance section is used here.

**S5 — Interior utility, spatial reassurance and return navigation.** Unknown Worlds, **“Coming Next: Seabases,” 2015-02-27**, official publisher post. [Announcement](https://steamcommunity.com/games/264710/announcements/detail/515997975634130855), full text via the Steam API. Relevant paragraphs: **Dive Reel**, prototype deck/window experience, compartment equipment, and repeated PDA opening/closing being “clunky.”

**Sourced:** a dive line helps retrace caves; developers describe the distinctive feeling of standing inside watching ocean life; the base prototype tested equipment placement and construction interaction before finished art. **Siltwake inference:** evaluate return-path comprehension and whether a Creel cabin or Hearthline room supports preparation, reflection or useful work with little repeated friction. **Limit:** this is author-reported prototype experience, not human evidence of comfort. Do not add a dive reel, freeform bases or compartment flooding because this source used them.

**S6 — Curiosity, clue structure and nonlinear comprehension.** **Kelsey Beachum**, Outer Wilds writer/narrative designer, **“Sparking Curiosity-Driven Exploration Through Narrative in Outer Wilds,” GDC 2021**. [Organizer-hosted slides, 81 PDF pages](https://media.gdcvault.com/GDC+2021/beachum_gdc_2021(1).pdf). Text inspected directly; the talk video was not watched. Page numbers below are one-based PDF pages. The deck contains Outer Wilds spoilers; the principles here omit its solutions.

**Sourced:** pp. 13–24 describe curiosity → exploration → knowledge and the game's specific choice to make knowledge its only reward. Pp. 28–38 describe physical phenomena as hooks and discoverable, player-requested information instead of frequent forced exposition; pp. 39–41 show optional starting guidance. Pp. 45–46 emphasize identifiable individuals and distinct voices. Pp. 52–60 describe clues serving story and navigation, links toward earlier/later portions of a thread, and a ship log recording only what the player certainly learned, without supplying inferences. Pp. 61–73 describe smaller arcs that work in different orders, layered discoverability and intermediate emotional/information payoffs.

**Siltwake inference:** a regional lead should leave a reason to investigate; a notebook should preserve evidence while distinguishing observation from hypothesis; a later clue should still orient someone who missed an earlier one. Small local mysteries can pay off before the central answer. **Limit:** Outer Wilds deliberately rejects equipment progression. Siltwake explicitly combines knowledge with crafting and pressure capability; removing those gates would violate its design. The source does not prescribe Siltwake's three independent foreshadowing clues, graduated hint implementation or pacing numbers; those come from the specification or proposed review methods.

**S7 — Stylization serving identification.** **Jason Mitchell, Moby Francke and Dhabih Eng**, Valve, **“Illustrative Rendering in Team Fortress 2,” 2007**. [Valve-hosted paper](https://cdn.akamai.steamstatic.com/apps/valve/2007/NPAR07_IllustrativeRenderingInTeamFortress2.pdf), especially **§3, §4.1–4.3, PDF pp. 2–3**.

**Sourced:** distinct silhouettes identify roles at different distances/viewpoints; internal shapes reinforce silhouettes; controlled detail and environmental value/color composition prevent visual noise from overpowering gameplay features. The paper connects artistic and shading choices to scene readability under varied lighting. **Siltwake inference:** test recognizable hulls, resource hosts, exits and warning poses from the real swimming/piloting camera through fog, silt and clutter. **Limit:** this is a multiplayer shooter art system, not underwater visibility evidence. It does not require TF2's palette, exact shader, low-detail style or rim-light solution. Richer cel detail should support Siltwake's own readable hierarchy.

**S8 — Redundant critical cues.** Microsoft Xbox Accessibility, **XAG 103: “Additional channels for visual and audio cues”**, displayed last update **2026-03-04**. [Guideline](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/xbox/accessibility/xbox-accessibility-guidelines/103); **Goal**, **Color vision deficiency**, **Implementation guidelines**. Full relevant text inspected.

**Sourced:** critical visual/audio information needs another sensory channel; color-based information also needs another signifier such as shape, pattern or text. Multiple visual indicators alone do not address blindness. Color-vision simulation is explicitly not a substitute for testing with affected players. **Siltwake inference:** examine directional hunter warnings, pressure/power alerts, resource identity and interaction feedback with audio off and with color cues unavailable. **Limit:** those diagnostic conditions do not establish full accessibility. A visual sound caption should communicate the heard cue, not expose hidden identity, exact coordinates or knowledge unavailable through the sound.

**S9 — Readable HUD and notebook.** Microsoft Xbox Accessibility, **XAG 101: “Text display”**, displayed last update **2026-03-04**. [Guideline](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/xbox/accessibility/xbox-accessibility-guidelines/101); **Measuring text size**, **Key areas**, **Implementation guidelines — Text size / Text scaling**.

**Sourced:** guidance covers prompts, menus, HUD and errors, not just dialogue. It recommends PC text body height of at least 18 pixels at 1080p and scaling to 200% without losing content/function; this measurement uses visible glyph height, not simply a CSS font-size value. **Siltwake inference:** inspect source hints, missing ingredients, suit/hull limits and emergency instructions at the largest offered text size in actual scenes. **Limit:** these are useful reference measurements, not newly adopted Siltwake acceptance thresholds; player needs and display conditions still require testing.

**S10 — Dialogue subtitles are not all sound information.** Microsoft Xbox Accessibility, **XAG 104: “Subtitles and captions”**, displayed last update **2026-03-04**. [Guideline](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/xbox/accessibility/xbox-accessibility-guidelines/104); **Overview** and **Implementation guidelines**, including initial settings, significant sounds, direction and placement.

**Sourced:** subtitles convey speech; captions can convey meaningful non-speech cues. Guidance includes making options available before the opening, indicating offscreen direction when needed, readable backgrounds, and preventing enlarged subtitles from covering important gameplay/UI. **Siltwake inference:** check the opening, environmental audio evidence, hazard warnings and refuge feedback with the actual mix and text enabled. **Limit:** the entire guideline is not automatically Siltwake scope; for example, this report does not add sign-language production, voice acting or a new platform.

Source IDs give the rationale, not proof of the candidate's behavior. Record the exact build and observation for every finding. ## Short evidence protocol for the later critic

1. **Freeze a reviewable campaign.** At M3 / early M4, the coordinator assigns a fresh non-author `gpt-6-astra` at `xhigh`, verifies the actual selection, and supplies one candidate SHA, base, specification, known gaps, normal-play saves and evidence. The critic must be distinct from implementation authors and this research worker. Inspect the production artifact corresponding to that SHA. This advisory design pass does not replace the required independent Astra/Sol pair or coordinator self-review.
2. **Protect first impressions.** Before reading spoiler canon or solution routes, the critic attempts the opening and a self-chosen expedition from New Game using ordinary controls, documenting the perceived question and choices. Afterwards, the authorized critic can inspect canon privately to audit chronology, corroboration and reveal order. That preserves an initial observation without pretending a model is a fresh human. Use spoiler-labelled detailed findings; keep ordinary summaries factual and spoiler-free.
3. **Sample relationships, then examine whole-run evidence.** Cover opening/first return; unfamiliar source and later revisit; alternate fleet mission; carrier interior/launch; threat/refuge; deep recovery; out-of-order clue/hint; and endgame payoff. A save reached through normal play can support a targeted replay, but does not count as a fresh start-to-ending run. Inspect both full strategy runs and reproduce consequential concerns through ordinary input. Debug state, teleports or direct commands remain labelled diagnostics. Do not claim complete campaign coverage from short excerpts.
4. **Keep one small observation ledger.** For each trace record `SHA / route or save provenance / settings / start-end timestamps / intended question / chosen preparation / observable action and result / clue or source ID / hint or intervention / evidence link / unresolved inference`. Separately tag active discovery, planning, first traversal, solved traversal, repeated harvesting, waiting, sorting, failure/recovery and pauses. Preserve raw durations and player explanations; a repeated trip can be satisfying mastery or tedious labor. No universal waste percentage is inferred from the research.
5. **Separate human evidence.** Seek fresh participants for full main-story runs, with different fleet choices where feasible, plus accessibility participants for relevant barriers. Let people choose naturally; use brief questions at safe pauses and a second-session recap. Record counts, prior familiarity, assistance and incomplete sessions. Small samples identify problems without proving population-wide satisfaction. If unavailable, leave duration, enjoyment, mystery payoff and relevant accessibility findings **unverified**, as the delivery document permits; do not rename agent walkthroughs human playtests.
6. **Turn observations into fixes and re-review.** Every finding names the player consequence, matrix row/spec clause, reproduction/evidence, confidence and a bounded remedy direction. Coordinator assigns fixes, freezes a revised SHA, and returns changed evidence plus every finding/disposition and unresolved objection to the critic. Reproduce the original problem and check adjacent route, lore, readability or recovery consequences. Use fresh humans for surprise/first-route claims that prior exposure invalidates. Before release, reconcile this pass with the independent acceptance gate: both non-author Astra xhigh and Sol 6.1 max must explicitly accept the same candidate, with exceptions still visible. For T3 reviews, create a new delegated task per round and retain task IDs; this research launches none.

Before any later browser/preview/screenshot/test: read `/work/repos/fleet/BROWSER_RULES.md` when available **and** `docs/BROWSER_RULES.md`; verify the isolated Linux factory-worker; prefer T3 `preview_status` then `preview_open`; preserve human/shared tabs including historical `tab14`; isolate storage and close only task-owned resources. Include these rules in the critic's brief. This research created no browser resources. Representative GPU measurements remain separate from software-rendered functional/art evidence.

## Severity and disposition

Suggested triage language for that future pass; this is not a numerical “fun” score or an amendment to acceptance authority. **Severity describes consequence; confidence describes evidence.** A hypothesis can deserve a test without being asserted as a defect.

| Classification | Consequence and example | Required disposition before claiming the relevant result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Blocker** | Required path cannot finish/recover; permanent knowledge or scarce-module loss; one mandated fleet strategy impossible; essential cue wholly unavailable with no usable alternative. | Reproduce, fix and re-review. Do not accept the affected campaign/criterion on a promise. |
| **Major** | A central promise repeatedly fails although an informed workaround exists: carrier has no useful campaign runway, most expeditions repeat harvesting, regional clues are unusable, or the reveal contradicts preserved evidence. | Correct and re-test the affected experience; any rejected finding needs specific specification/evidence reasoning, not reviewer preference. Unresolved scope/correctness disputes remain open under the existing gate. |
| **Moderate** | A localized problem damages a mission: one hull's intended advantage is obscured, an important caption collides with a prompt, or a useful alternate path is poorly signalled. | Assign a bounded fix and replay that context. Escalate if it recurs, excludes a player, or removes the only route. |
| **Minor** | Limited polish friction with a clear, functioning route: inconsistent optional label or noncritical art distraction. | Fix where practical or record a specific remaining limitation; do not inflate it to a campaign verdict. |
| **Evidence gap** | Required observation was never made: no fresh human duration, no relevant accessibility participants, or no representative GPU run. | State exactly what remains unverified. This is neither a low-severity defect nor a pass. Apply only the exceptions the existing delivery gate actually permits. |
| **Design hypothesis** | Plausible improvement without observed failure: a different landmark, recipe quantity or sound mix might work better. | Propose a discriminating playtest. Do not turn preference or another game's mechanic into new scope. |

The critic's eventual handoff should identify what works and why, prioritized defects with evidence, unresolved hypotheses, exact tested SHA and remaining human/hardware gaps. Include fix-and-re-review dispositions rather than a generic rating. This report supplies the questions and rationale; it supplies **no verdict on Siltwake's current or eventual play quality**.
