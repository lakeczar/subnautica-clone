# LS-1 lost sites: lore appendix

**MAJOR STORY SPOILERS: writers and integration agents only.** Companion to [NARRATIVE_CANON](NARRATIVE_CANON.md) and [CONTENT_BIBLE_LORE](CONTENT_BIBLE_LORE.md); both win on conflict. The owner-facing plan is [LS-1](../build/streams/LS-1.md). Its text, the data, the IDs, the labels and all player strings stay twist-free. Written by the LS-1 lore and world integration worker (Opus 5.5, 2026-10-24).

The six rules in CONTENT_BIBLE_LORE ("Rules every lost site follows") apply to every row below without exception. In short: nothing human is dated after disaster day; no counts, no suspension, no refuge below, no six, no gate order or pressure trace; every decode is `story: false`; there are exactly 20 + 6 deaths; and the cause is physical.

The owner-facing plan may say only what the owner-facing bible already says: extraction was logged, the slope and the warnings were called drift, and the crews left in a hurry. It never says why, where they went, who decided, or what the machinery protects.
